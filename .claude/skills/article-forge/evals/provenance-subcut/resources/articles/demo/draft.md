# Your staff already use AI. Your policy should assume it.

AI at work stopped being an experiment some time ago. A 2026 survey by Acme Research found that 61% of office workers now use generative AI tools at least weekly [UNVERIFIED], and at large firms that figure rises to 74% [UNVERIFIED].

That changes the policy question. It is no longer "should we allow it?" but "how do we govern what is already happening?"

Start with visibility. You cannot set sensible rules for tools you cannot see.
