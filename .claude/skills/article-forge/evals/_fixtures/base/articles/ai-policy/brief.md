# Brief: ai-policy

Status: LOCKED (approved by Sam)

- Core argument: banning AI tools at work moves the risk out of sight; govern use instead.
- Conclusion (verbatim intent): "Write the policy for the tools people already use."
- Audience: IT and security leaders at mid-sized companies. Secondary: CFOs.
- Outcome: comments from peers sharing their own policy experience.
- Positioning: no vendor mentions.
- Register: default. Length: 800 to 1,000 words.
- Anchors: I helped roll out an AI usage policy at my previous employer; I was one of three people on that project.
- Repeat line: "A ban is a policy for the tools you can see."
