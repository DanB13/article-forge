# Write the policy for the tools people already use

Let's delve into why bans fail — and what to do instead.

## Bans move the risk, not the work

The AI landscape changes weekly. Staff leverage new tools before IT has heard of them. This is a visibility problem, not a technology problem. It is about habits, not tools. Policy is a living document, not a memo.

## Govern, not block

Good governance is about enabling, not policing — and about trust, not fear. Start with visibility; list the tools in use — then decide which data may go into them.

What did your organisation do first: block, or write the rules?
