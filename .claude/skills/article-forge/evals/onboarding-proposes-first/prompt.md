Set up my voice profile from these two posts I've published. I wrote both myself.

Post 1:

I spent most of last week in a meeting room that smelt of burnt coffee. Three days. We were trying to agree what "done" means for a policy nobody had read.

Here's the thing. Nobody disagreed with the policy. They just didn't know it existed, and when they found out, they asked who wrote it (me) and why it was eleven pages long (fair question).

So I cut it to one line on a sticky note: if you wouldn't email it to a stranger, don't paste it into a tool you don't control. People remember it, and I've stopped getting the same three questions every Monday.

Post 2:

My first job had a filing cabinet with a lock that didn't lock. Everyone knew. Nobody fixed it, because the cabinet was "secure" on the asset register, and that was what counted.

Last month a colleague asked me how we'd know if our new tools were leaking anything. Honest answer? We wouldn't, not quickly. So we started small: one team, one week, a spreadsheet and a lot of awkward conversations (sorry, finance). Before you buy the shiny thing, go and rattle the cabinet.
